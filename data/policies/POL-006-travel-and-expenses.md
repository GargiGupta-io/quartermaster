# POL-006 Travel and Expenses

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 6.1 Scope

This policy covers employee travel and reimbursable expenses. It does not apply to
purchases of goods, which follow POL-001.

## 6.2 Approval limits

- Trips up to ₹25,000: Line Manager.
- Trips above ₹25,000: Department Head.

## 6.3 Reimbursement

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense, with receipts for any amount above
₹500. Accessories such as laptop bags up to ₹3,000 may be claimed as an expense.
