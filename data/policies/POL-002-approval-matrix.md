# POL-002 Approval Matrix

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 2.1 Approval limits

Approval depends on the order total including GST. Each level includes the approvers
named for that level only.

- Up to ₹50,000: Line Manager.
- ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000: Department Head.
- ₹5,00,001 to ₹25,00,000: Department Head and Finance Controller.
- Above ₹25,00,000: Department Head, Finance Controller and CFO.

## 2.2 Additional approvals

Regardless of value, the Finance Controller must approve any order placed in a foreign
currency. The CIO must approve any hardware exception under POL-004.

## 2.3 No splitting

An order must not be split into smaller orders to fall under a lower approval limit.
Items requested together for the same purpose count as one order.
