# POL-001 Procurement Policy

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 1.1 Scope

This policy applies to every purchase of goods or services made with company funds.
Travel and employee expenses are covered by POL-006 instead.

## 1.2 Use existing stock first

Before buying, stock available at the requested office must be used. Available stock
means units on hand minus units already reserved. Only the shortfall may be purchased.

If another office has enough available stock to cover the entire shortfall, the
items must be transferred rather than bought. A partial transfer is not required.

## 1.3 Budget

A purchase may only be raised against the budget of the requesting department.
Remaining budget means allocated budget minus amounts spent and amounts committed.

If the total including GST exceeds the remaining budget, the request cannot proceed
without a budget exception approved by the CFO.

## 1.4 Tax

All approval limits and budget checks use the total including GST. GST is charged at
18% on all goods in this policy unless the purchase order states otherwise.

## 1.5 Prohibited purchases

The following may not be purchased through procurement under any circumstances:
gift cards and vouchers, cryptocurrency, personal items for employees, and alcohol.

## 1.6 Purchase orders

No goods may be ordered without a purchase order. A purchase order may only be created
after every approval required by POL-002 has been recorded.
