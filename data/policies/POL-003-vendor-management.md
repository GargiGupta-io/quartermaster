# POL-003 Vendor Management

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 3.1 Vendor status

- Approved: may receive orders of any value.
- Probation: may receive orders up to ₹5,00,000 including GST per order.
- Suspended: may not receive orders or be used as a quote.

Vendors not on the vendor list may not receive orders.

## 3.2 Competitive quotes

The number of quotes required depends on the order total including GST.

- Up to ₹1,00,000: one quote.
- ₹1,00,001 to ₹10,00,000: two quotes.
- Above ₹10,00,000: three quotes.

Quotes must come from vendors with Approved status that serve the delivery office.

If fewer qualifying vendors exist than the number required, the order may proceed as a
sole-source purchase, and the reason must be recorded on the purchase order.

## 3.3 Vendor selection

Among vendors eligible for the order, the lowest price excluding GST must be chosen.
A higher-priced vendor may be chosen only if the lowest-priced vendor cannot meet a
delivery date stated in the request, and the reason must be recorded.
