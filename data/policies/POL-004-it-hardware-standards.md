# POL-004 IT Hardware Standards

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 4.1 Laptop tiers

Price caps are per unit, excluding GST.

- Standard (LAP-STD-14): all employees. Cap ₹85,000.
- Engineering (LAP-ENG-16): employees in the Engineering department only. Cap ₹1,40,000.

## 4.2 Hardware exceptions

A laptop above the cap for its tier, or a tier the employee's department is not entitled
to, requires a hardware exception approved by the CIO. The request must state a business
reason. LAP-PRO-16 is above every cap and always requires an exception.

## 4.3 Accessories

Each new employee may receive one monitor (MON-27) and one dock (DOCK-USBC).
