# POL-005 Foreign Currency Purchases

Kestrel Works Pvt Ltd is a fictional company. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 5.1 When allowed

A vendor billing in a foreign currency may be used only when no Approved vendor billing
in INR can supply the item to the delivery office. A lower price is not a valid reason.

## 5.2 Conversion

Convert the price to INR at the current market rate on the day of the request, then add
a 3% buffer for exchange-rate movement. Budget and approval checks use this buffered
amount plus GST.

## 5.3 Approval

Foreign currency orders always require Finance Controller approval, as stated in POL-002.
